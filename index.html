🎓 Flight Tips
  • Pronk for height, then burn sideways. Sprint to about SPD 180 and pronk: your high point (Ap) lands near 200. Coast up to it and boost until Pe clears the ground and the path turns green.
  • Let LEVEL fly the nose. With Level assist on, let go of ◀ ▶ and the nose snaps flat, so Boost burns straight along your orbit. Tap the direction you're flying first to speed up; tap the other way to slow down.
  • Red line = trouble. LOST or ESCAPE means the goat will drift away. Turn around and burn to slow down.
  • Save fuel. Stars come from beating the par time and the Δv par. A headbutt nudge can push a herd-mate into orbit for free.
  • Rounds rotate: full orbit → altitude band → ring gates. Moss refuels you and grows kids.
  • 📦 Supply crates (round 2+): match the crate's orbit and drift into it slowly (under 80 relative speed) for a full tank and +150 points. To catch up with something ahead of you, slow down: a lower orbit is faster.
  • 🛰️ Space debris (round 5+): a pulsing ⚠ marker shows where a collision would happen and how soon. A short burn changes your path and dodges it.
  • 🐐 Stampede: one endless run. Each round adds a goat and tightens par by 4%. You have 3 ❤️ herd hearts: every goat lost or abducted costs one. Lose all three and the run is over. No retries!
  • 🐑 Sheep (round 2+): they stay on the ground and flock. Clustered sheep are nearly immovable; a lone sheep panics, and a slam near it makes it curl into a rolling woolball. Sheep grazing moss earn steady forage points. If 3+ sheep outnumber you at a moss patch, they'll ram you along the ground.
  • 🟠 Copper Mineral: a goat that touches it gets a speed and jump boost. A sheep that eats it gets Copper Toxicity, so grab it first.
  • 🌵 Spire-shrubs: fly through the tip of a shrub on a crater rim for a +75 trick-shot bonus.
  • 🎖️ Goat ranks: every orbit and every round cleared earns a goat XP. Goats rank up from Kid to Billy, Astronaut, Commander and Astro-Goat, and get new gear at each rank.
  • Hazards arrive later: meteor strikes start in round 3, UFO abductions in round 4 and space debris in round 5 (meteors and UFO arrive in rounds 2 and 3 of the Daily). Headbutt the UFO to stop it.
